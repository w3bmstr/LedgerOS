About LedgerOS

LedgerOS v5.0 — complete privacy-first bookkeeping software for freelancers and small businesses.

Runs entirely in your browser (localStorage). Full double-entry foundation, payroll, estimates, bills, banking, inventory & more.

Features: Chart of Accounts · Multi-line Journal · Clients · Estimates · Invoices (multi-currency) · Vendors · Purchase Orders · Bills · Expenses · Inventory & COGS · Employees · Payroll · Banking & Reconciliation · Tax Center (GST/HST) · Fixed Assets · Receipts with OCR · Recurring Automation · Cash Flow Forecasting · AI Accountant · Budgets · Spreadsheet · Multi-Business · Audit Trail · Roles · Document Attachments · PDF Export · File-based Sync · Full Reports · Dark mode · PWA / Offline
