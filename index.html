About LedgerOS

LedgerOS v2.1 — a modern, privacy-first accounting workspace for freelancers and small businesses.

Currently runs entirely in your browser (localStorage). Perfect for personal use or as a foundation for a full SaaS product.

Features: Dashboard · Clients · Invoices (with recurring) · Expenses · Reports · Budget · Spreadsheet · Dark mode · PWA / Installable · Offline-ready
