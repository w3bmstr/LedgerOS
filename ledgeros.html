About LedgerOS

LedgerOS v3.0 — complete privacy-first bookkeeping software for freelancers and small businesses.

Runs entirely in your browser (localStorage). Full double-entry foundation, payroll, estimates, bills & more.

Features: Chart of Accounts · Journal · Clients · Estimates · Invoices · Vendors · Bills · Expenses · Employees · Payroll · Budgets · Spreadsheet · Full Reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Aging) · Dark mode · PWA / Offline
